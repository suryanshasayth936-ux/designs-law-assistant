### Q1. How long does a proprietor have copyright in a registered design, and can it be extended?
**Answer:** A proprietor has copyright in a registered design for ten years from the date of registration. This period can be extended by five years if the registered proprietor applies for extension in the prescribed manner before the expiration of the original ten-year period (Reference: Chapter 6, Section 11, Page 36; Chapter 7, Section 11(2), Form 3, Page 39).

### Q2. Which classification system is used to register designs?
**Answer:** Designs are classified under the Third Schedule of the Design Rules, 2001 (which contains thirty-one classes and a miscellaneous class 99) based on the Locarno Classification system / International Classification of Industrial Designs (Reference: Chapter 1 – Introduction, Page 5; Chapter 3 – Filing of Design Application, Form-1, Third Schedule, Page 14, 20).
